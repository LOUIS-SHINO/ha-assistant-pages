隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

本應用程式僅由擁有者本人用於自己的 Home Assistant，將文字指令傳送給 Google Assistant。不蒐集、不儲存、不分享任何其他使用者的資料；授權憑證只存在擁有者自己的 Home Assistant 中。

This app is used solely by its owner to send text commands from the owner's own Home Assistant to Google Assistant. It does not collect, store, or share any data about other users. OAuth tokens are kept only in the owner's own Home Assistant instance.
